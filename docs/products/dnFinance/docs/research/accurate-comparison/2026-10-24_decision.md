# dnFinance vs Accurate Online — product and roadmap comparison

*Generated: 2026-10-24 | Scope: dnFinance repository snapshot + public Accurate Online evidence accessed 2026-10-24*

## TL;DR

dnFinance is credible on the accounting core, Indonesian tax primitives, multi-tenant access, approvals, and API-first foundations. Accurate is ahead on breadth, maturity of operational modules, integrations, help content, and ecosystem packaging—especially bank reconciliation, fixed assets, manufacturing, POS, and 200+ report positioning.

The move is not to clone Accurate. Harden the core and win a narrower job: let small Indonesian teams get from setup to trustworthy monthly close, approvals, and owner-readable cash insight with less accounting complexity.

## What dnFinance should build next

### P0 — trust and close reliability

1. Finish normalized Postgres persistence and migration checks; remove production dependence on JSON snapshots.
2. Make real email, queue, retry, and scheduled-report delivery observable and replayable.
3. Add reconciliation-safe period close: lock/close period, adjustment journal, audit trail, and immutable posted entries.
4. Ship a responsive owner dashboard with cash, overdue AR/AP, upcoming bills, and “what changed this month.”

### P1 — highest-value parity gaps

1. Bank statement import first, then matching rules and an unmatched queue; treat live bank connectivity as a later connector.
2. Fixed asset register with acquisition, useful life, method, depreciation schedule, disposal, and GL integration.
3. Stronger tax workflow: period checklist, evidence attachments, export validation, and explicit regulation-versioning.
4. Improve import/migration UX from Excel with preview, mapping, validation errors, and rollback.

### P2 — selective differentiation and vertical bets

1. Deterministic finance insights: largest expense, variance vs prior period, overdue concentration, and recommended action with source transactions.
2. Connectors for POS/e-commerce rather than building a POS suite immediately.
3. Manufacturing only after customer evidence proves a production segment; scope BOM/formula, material issue, WIP, finished goods, and variance as a separate package.
4. AI assistant only with citations to reports/transactions, permission-aware retrieval, and a human-verifiable answer trail.

## Positioning recommendation

Target initially: Indonesian service, retail, and small trading companies with 1–3 finance users that need formal books and approvals but do not want an ERP implementation project.

Message to test: **“Tutup buku lebih cepat, tanpa harus jadi ahli akuntansi.”**

Proof points should be concrete: industry COA setup, approval links, owner dashboard, scheduled reports, bank matching, and explainable insights. Avoid claiming Accurate-level breadth or production maturity until the conditional infrastructure is live.

## Product research plan

This comparison creates hypotheses, not user insights. Run evaluative usability sessions with three segments: owner/operator, accountant, and manager/approver. Use six participants per segment for moderate problem-discovery coverage. Tasks: register/setup COA, import opening balances, create invoice/bill, approve, reconcile a statement, and read a month-end report. Separately run a live preference/pricing experiment; do not infer market prevalence from usability n.

## Market-research note

No TAM/SAM/SOM is included. A defensible size requires both top-down and bottom-up inputs. The next sizing step should use paid Indonesian SME accounting buyers as the market unit, segment by workflow complexity (cashbook, formal books, inventory, multi-branch/manufacturing), and document reachable channels and willingness-to-pay instead of treating “all UMKM” as one segment.

## Risks and caveats

- Accurate price and packaging can change; refresh official pricing before launch or pricing decisions.
- Public vendor pages are claims, not independent usability evidence.
- dnFinance docs have different snapshot dates; reconcile the feature catalog, implementation baseline, and actual deployed environment before publishing a parity claim.
- The independent articles are useful positioning signals but not a substitute for interviews or product telemetry.
