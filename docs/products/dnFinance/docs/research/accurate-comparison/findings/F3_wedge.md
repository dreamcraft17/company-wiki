# F3 — Recommended wedge: simpler control plane for small teams

Confidence: medium; this is a positioning hypothesis requiring validation.

Independent Indonesian comparison articles repeatedly frame Accurate as formal/deep and suitable for businesses with stronger accounting and tax needs, while also noting that its menu structure can feel complex for beginners. Accurate's official packaging also makes expansion costs visible through per-user/data-unit licensing and add-ons.

dnFinance can test a wedge around: fast setup from an industry COA, clean approval flows, tenant-safe collaboration, scheduled reports, actionable cash/expense insights, and a mobile-friendly owner dashboard. The promise should be “finance control without an accounting maze,” not “Accurate with fewer features.”
