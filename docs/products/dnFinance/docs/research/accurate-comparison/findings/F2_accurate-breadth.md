# F2 — Accurate wins on breadth and ecosystem depth

Confidence: high for advertised module breadth.

Accurate's official surface includes sales, purchasing, cash/bank, inventory, fixed assets, financial reports, manufacturing, POS, Trade Portal, tax, and 2FA/help content. Its bank workflow includes statement retrieval/import and reconciliation; fixed assets include depreciation and book value; manufacturing includes formula, raw-material issue, finished goods, and variance handling.

dnFinance should not chase all of this before hardening its core. Fixed assets, bank reconciliation, and stronger operational integrations are the most relevant gaps for a general SME accounting product. Manufacturing is a distinct vertical bet.
