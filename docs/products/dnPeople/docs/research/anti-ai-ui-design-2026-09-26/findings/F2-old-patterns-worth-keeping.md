# F2 — Keep the useful parts of older enterprise/web UI

Older interfaces were often more explicit: borders separated regions, controls looked actionable, labels stayed visible, and tables/ledgers exposed state. NN/G’s clickability work confirms that visible signifiers reduce uncertainty; its enterprise guidance confirms that consistency and in-context workflow support matter more than novelty.

Design implication: use rectangular/low-radius control groups, table-like evidence rows, section rails, tabs with clear selected states, and restrained shadows. Do not copy bevels, glossy buttons, GIF-era decoration, or fixed-width desktop layouts.
