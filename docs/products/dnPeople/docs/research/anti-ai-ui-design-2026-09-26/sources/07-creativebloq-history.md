## Relevant evidence

The history describes the 2000s “touch and feel” period, Gmail’s clean white interface, the 2010s flattening of surfaces, Material Design’s explicit system, and the later revival of optimistic/physical visual languages. It shows that older interfaces had recognizable identities, but also that every era had excesses.

## Use in decision

The useful dnPeople direction is “practical web software with a point of view”: paper-like surfaces, clear rails, restrained color, explicit controls, and editorial evidence. Avoid literal nostalgia such as faux desktop chrome or decorative glossy effects.
