# Source 04 — Nielsen Norman Group: Flat design and clickable elements

- URLs: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/flat-design/ and https://www.nngroup.com/articles/clickable-elements/
- Type: usability research/practice guidance
- Retrieved: 2026-09-26
- Credibility: high for signifiers and interaction clarity.

## Relevant evidence

NN/G describes “Flat 2.0” as a compromise: mostly flat, with restrained layers/shadows that help users read hierarchy. It warns that completely flat interfaces can remove clickability clues. Its clickability guidance emphasizes recognizable signifiers, clear link/button treatment, and reducing click uncertainty.

Short quote: “Stripping away too much undermines this objective by making the interaction more complex.”

## Use in decision

Do not replace AI slop with a borderless, ultra-flat UI. Keep visible borders, selected states, underlines or button fills, and restrained elevation only where they communicate interaction or hierarchy.
