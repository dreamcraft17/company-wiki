# Source 03 — Nielsen Norman Group: Enterprise Usability

- URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/enterprise-usability/
- Type: usability research/practice guidance
- Retrieved: 2026-09-26
- Credibility: high for enterprise UX guidance; article is older and should be complemented by current product testing.

## Relevant evidence

Enterprise usability is not just an individual click task. It includes long-running workflows, group coordination, consistency, support burden, and in-context field studies. NN/G specifically recommends observing real work and notes that design standards improve consistency and help-desk productivity.

Short quote: “Design standards help individual users by enforcing UI consistency.”

## Use in decision

dnPeople should look like a dependable work instrument: stable naming, visible states, predictable tables/forms, and workflow evidence. A distinctive visual language must not create new training cost.
