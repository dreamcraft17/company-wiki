# Plan — dnPeople product–market reset

> **Author:** Dozer  
> **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Reframe

**Pertanyaan asli:** "Apakah dnPeople produk yang salah? Belum ada klien."

**Keputusan di balik pertanyaan:** Dapatkan bukti pasar (design partner / paying pilot) sebelum menambah modul v16+.

**Hipotesis (falsifiable):**

1. **H1 — Produk category salah:** SME Indonesia tidak butuh HRIS baru; pasar sudah jenuh Talenta/Gadjian → *refute jika 3+ design partner retail/F&B setuju pain wedge kita.*
2. **H2 — Produk scope salah:** Breadth HRIS (129 model) bukan wedge; buyer butuh "HRIS lengkap" → *refute jika pilot menang lewat workflow audit + payroll review, bukan demo modul.*
3. **H3 — GTM salah (prioritas):** Zero klien karena tidak ada founder-led outbound, ICP terlalu luas, dan CTA hanya form email → *confirm jika 90 hari outbound + WhatsApp audit menghasilkan pipeline.*
4. **H4 — Harga/posisi:** Rp10–20k/karyawan undercut tapi tanpa trust proof tidak cukup vs incumbent → *test dengan 2 pilot berbayar STARTER/PRO.*

## Genre

**Decision brief** + **validation playbook** (bukan landscape report).

## Scope

- Internal: FEATURE-CATALOG, research 2026-09-19, BD panduan, landing `/welcome`.
- External: competitor messaging (Talenta, GreatDay), SME HR penetration (secondary sources).

## Out of scope

- Build Module 4–8 (EWA, marketplace, benchmarking).
- Native app commit.
- Klaim TAM angka tunggal tanpa bottoms-up.

## Stop criteria

- 2 design partner retail/F&B onboarded **or**
- 30 workflow audits completed with go/no-go log **or**
- Explicit kill/pivot decision documented.

## Sourcing strategy

| Sub-question | Source type |
|--------------|-------------|
| Apakah wedge sudah benar? | Internal F1 + competitor pages |
| Kenapa belum klien? | Internal GTM gap + product-research method |
| Apa yang dikerjakan 90 hari? | marketing-ideas + BD playbook |
| Apakah pasar SME masih terbuka? | Industry secondary (penetration <25% SME) |

## Opposition queries

- "Talenta sudah menang SME — why another HRIS?"
- "Spreadsheet cukup untuk 50 orang."
- "Tim kecil tidak punya waktu migrasi."

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Build lebih banyak fitur alih-alih sell | Freeze v16; KPI = conversations + pilots |
| Persona retail/F&B belum validasi | 12 interviews before scaling ads |
| Early release unlimited gratis tanpa feedback | Kontrak feedback + exit criteria |
