## Implication

BPS provides a route to build a bottoms-up market model by industry, company size, geography, and workforce, but the current sources are insufficient to quote a dnPeople TAM/SAM/SOM. A future sizing pass must join the relevant establishment counts to qualified ICP penetration and annual price assumptions.
