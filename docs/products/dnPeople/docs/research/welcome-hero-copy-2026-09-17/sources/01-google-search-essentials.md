# Source 01 — Google Search Essentials

- **URL:** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials
- **Type:** Primary / official search documentation
- **Accessed:** 2026-09-17
- **Relevance:** Guidance on language users search for, prominent placement, crawlable links, and people-first content.

## Verbatim excerpts

> “Create helpful, reliable, people-first content.”

> “Use words that people would use to look for your content, and place those words in prominent locations on the page.”

## Application

The page should use natural category language in prominent visible content, but the eyebrow does not need to carry every keyword. The page's H1 and supporting copy are better places for category terms such as HRIS, absensi, and payroll.
