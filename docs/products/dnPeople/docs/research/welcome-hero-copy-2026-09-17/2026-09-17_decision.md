## TL;DR

Set the hero eyebrow to **`Untuk perusahaan multi-cabang`**. Keep the category and qualification terms in the title/subheading: HRIS multi-cabang, absensi, shift, approval, payroll BPJS/PPh 21, audit trail HR, and 50–300 karyawan.

This wording best balances audience clarity, market relevance, scanability, and dnPeople’s current differentiation. It is not evidence of conversion lift; usability validation remains required.

## Decision context

The current H1 is:

> **Aturan HR pusat, tetap rapi di setiap cabang.**

The eyebrow should prepare the reader for this promise, not repeat the H1 or turn into a keyword list. dnPeople’s current supporting copy already explains the category and size range.

## Evidence synthesis

### Hypothesis H1 — audience + context improves orientation

**Directionally supported, not measured.** Google and homepage usability guidance both support clear, concise, customer-oriented language. The competitor landscape shows that generic HRIS/payroll language is already crowded. ### Hypothesis H3 — 50–300 is too much for the eyebrow

**Editorially supported; not empirically proven.** The phrase is valuable for qualification, but its placement in a long eyebrow reduces scanability and is not necessary because the supporting copy and metadata carry it.

### Hypothesis H4 — wording needs validation

**Confirmed.** No source can prove conversion impact for dnPeople’s specific audience. Run moderated usability testing or an A/B test.

## Adversarial review

1. **Could “perusahaan” be less specific than “tim HR”?** Yes, but the documented buying context is a company problem involving multiple roles. Keep HR/GA/finance/owner explicit in supporting copy and qualification fields.
2. **Is “multi-cabang” generic among competitors?** Yes. Mitigation: preserve the specific H1 and proof around approval, exceptions, payroll review, and evidence.
3. **Could “50–300” be an important qualifier?** Yes, but it belongs in metadata/supporting copy and pricing context rather than the eyebrow.
4. **Could the proposed wording improve ranking?** Not directly proven. It improves clarity architecture; ranking impact requires Search Console observation and controlled measurement.

## Validation plan
