# F1 — Recommended Hero Eyebrow

## Finding

Use **`Untuk perusahaan multi-cabang`** as the visible eyebrow above the dnPeople H1.

## Why

- “Perusahaan” identifies the buying context broadly enough to include owner/management, HR/GA, finance/payroll, and branch operations—not only the functional HR user.
- “Multi-cabang” identifies the operational context where dnPeople has its strongest wedge.
- It is short enough to scan and does not compete with the differentiating H1.
- It leaves category keywords—HRIS, absensi, payroll, BPJS/PPh 21, audit trail—in the supporting copy and metadata.

## Triangulation

- Google recommends using words people use to search in prominent content, but also recommends concise titles; this supports distributing keywords across the opening content rather than forcing all of them into the eyebrow. [01](../sources/01-google-search-essentials.md) [02](../sources/02-google-title-links-snippets.md)
- Nielsen Norman Group recommends a tagline that communicates customer value/differentiation and keeps high-priority tasks clear; this supports a short audience/context label above a stronger H1. [03](../sources/03-nng-homepage-usability.md)
- Competitor pages use HRIS, payroll, attendance, and multi-company language extensively; “multi-cabang” alone is not enough differentiation, so the H1 and proof must carry the operational-control story. [04](../sources/04-mekari-talenta.md) [05](../sources/05-greatday-hr.md) [06](../sources/06-gadjian.md)

## Confidence

**Medium editorial confidence.** The direction is supported by three source types and is better aligned with the documented buying committee, but the effect on comprehension or conversion is not measured. Validate with usability testing or an A/B test.
