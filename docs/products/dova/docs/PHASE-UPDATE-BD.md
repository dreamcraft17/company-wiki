## 1. What DOVA is (for client conversations)

**DOVA** connects **business buyers** with **verified local food suppliers**.

- Buyers browse products, order (pickup or delivery), and pay online.  
- Suppliers register with documents, get approved, manage catalog/stock, and fulfill orders.  
- Admins approve suppliers and oversee the platform.

**MVP goal:** Prove the model with a working catalog, verified suppliers, and successful **test** payments — then soft-launch.

**Currency:** Nigerian Naira (₦) · **Payments:** Paystack
